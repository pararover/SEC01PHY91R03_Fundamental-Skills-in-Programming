\section{Applications}
	Applications of simple mechanical models provide immediate insight into oscillations, energy landscapes and dissipative behaviour encountered across physics and engineering. The plots and short scripts below illustrate three common topics: ideal (undamped) oscillatory motion which highlights sinusoidal time-dependence and frequency, damping curves that show how dissipation changes amplitude and introduces decay rates (underdamped, critically damped and overdamped cases), and potential-well profiles that visualise the forces and equilibrium points experienced by a particle.

	\subsection{Damping curves}
		Given below are the analytic solutions for a linear damped oscillator with mass $m$, damping coefficient $c$ and stiffness $k$. Using $\gamma=\dfrac{c}{2m}$ and $\omega_0=\dfrac{k}{m}$, we have
		\begin{itemize}
			\item Underdamping: $\gamma<\omega_0$
			\item Critical damping: $\gamma=\omega_0$
			\item Overdamping: $\gamma>\omega_0$
		\end{itemize}
